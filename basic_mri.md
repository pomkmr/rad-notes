# Basic Principles of MSK MRI
- Signal is the amount of information on an image. It's deteremined by slice thickness, field-of-view and the imaging matrix.
- The imaging matrix is the number of voxels in the grid.
- Due to **cross-talk**, if the slices are too close together,
there may be bleed of information into adjacent slices. Therefore a gap of 10-20% is often added.
- **Pulse Sequence** is a collection of specific imaging parameters for a single scan.
- Fast spin echo: T1, T2, PD. Gradient Echo: T2*, STIR.
- T1 is good for anatomic detail, but has poor detection of oedema or marrow pathology (compared to STIR/FSE).
- PD is good for anatomic detail and meniscal pathology. Same weaknesses as T1 for oedema / marrow.
- T2 is good for fluid and pathology. Excellent for marrow pathology with FS.
- T2* for fibrocartilagnous structures such as the meniscus or labrum.
Due to susceptibility effects, its very good for loose bodies and blood.
- STIR for marrow and soft tissue pathology.

## MSK Tissues and Best Sequences for Evaluation
### Bone
- Cortex is black on all sequences as the protons are too tightly packed to produce a signal.
- In the medullary cavity, fat and blood marrow are identified. Haematopoeitic marrow is slightly hypo to fat on T1 and mildly hyper to muscle on all sequences. 
- Most useful sequences are: STIR, FSE-T2 and T1. Note that the FSE-T2 should be fat suppressed otherwise pathology may be missed.
Both fat and pathological lesions will have high intensity on non-FS images

### Articular Cartilage
- STIR / FS-FSE-T2 shows dark cartilage, delineating it against bright fluid.
- The appearance varies on sequence used.

### Fibrocartilage
- Is dark on all sequences.
- Meniscus are shown best on FSE-PD, T2* and T1, all short TE sequences.
- Most meniscal tears are not seen on long TE (T2) images.
- Glenoid or acetabular labrum use T1 post-contrast.

### Tendons
- Are dark on all sequences, except ACL which has a striated appearance.
- Carful of the magic angle (55deg) which can cause artefacts in tendons.

### Muscle
- T1 is best for general look and atrophy of muscles. Fatty atrophy leads to brigter signal in muscles.
